03 — PROLOGUE

THE DIFFICULTY OF SAYING I LOVE YOU

I have never felt that if I were to die the next second, there would be anything to regret in this moment.

我从来不觉得，如果下一秒死去，此刻会有什么遗憾。

UNTIL I REALIZED—

I had never told my friends or my parents: “I love you.”

我一直都没有对朋友和父母说过“我爱你”。

DOES THAT MEAN
I DON'T LOVE THEM?

难道这代表我不爱他们吗？

QUITE THE OPPOSITE.

恰恰相反。

They are the largest part of who I am. The feeling is there. The sentence simply cannot cross the distance between the heart and the mouth.

他们是组成我的最大一部分。感情一直在，只是句子无法越过从心到嘴的距离。

In romance I said it too many times. Elsewhere, almost never. I open my messages and the few scattered “I love you”s are all from friends—not sent by me.

IS IT TO SAY IT?
